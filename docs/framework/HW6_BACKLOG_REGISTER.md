## Why these are not blockers

Every item is either (a) gated on LICENSED external content (SNOMED, CMS V28) — the NS-05 ceiling,
not a code gap; or (b) a bounded fidelity refinement to a subsystem that already functions with its
seeded/reference data (the demo and the Phase 1–2 controls do not depend on them). A pilot at a health
plan is safe without HW6; these raise policy fidelity toward a full production terminology/privacy posture.

_*Client-delivery category (WPC-CD*):_* items that are correct as server-side/stopgap today and only become priorities if/when the relevant capability is moved to client delivery. WPC-CD1 is the first such item.

## Recommended sequencing

Do the self-contained ones first when scheduled (HW6-2 ICD rollup, HW6-5 UCUM, HW6-7 tiebreak table),
then the content-gated ones (HW6-1/3/4) as licensed releases are provisioned, then the privacy-depth
items (HW6-8/9) alongside the HW2 audit/disclosure breadth follow-on.
