Golden Thread · First End-to-End Showcase · Storyboard v1 (coalition-hardened)

Maria Redhawk's PA Story

One member, one urgent prior authorization, threaded through the whole simulation — so the thesis lands in the room and survives the skeptic: evidence earns the machine authority; a clinician still owns every clinical “no”; and when the plan misses a clock, the miss is caught, contained, and can’t be hidden.

Aligned, illustrative — NIST does not certify AI systems; not a conformance assessment Seed data · mock channel — not transmitted; member & provider fictional PHI-safe — codes/refs only; clinical specifics abstract

A clinician marks Maria’s service expedited. Her plan has 72 hours from receipt to decide. It pends for a missing record — and the clock keeps running. Two endings are possible, and the demo tells the truth about both: the system catches the jeopardy and gets her a real decision in time — or it misses, and the safety net makes the miss a governed, appealable, fully-visible event rather than a silent denial.

The member — drawn with dignity, protections first

MR

Maria Redhawk (illustrative)

American Indian / Alaska Native · voluntarily enrolled in a Medicaid MCO (UnitedHealthcare Community Plan, seed) — permissible under SSA §1932(a)(2)(C), which exempts AI/AN members from mandatory managed-care enrollment. Urgent, time-sensitive medical service (not a drug, not SUD-related). Rendering site: an I/T/U-affiliated clinic (illustrative).
The story is built on what protects her, not on her being the one who gets denied:
I/T/U access preserved in- or out-of-network — 42 CFR 438.14 Language access — §1557 / 45 CFR Part 92 (interpreter, taglines on the NABD) Fairness screen works for her — §1557 four-fifths Timeliness safeguard — 438.210(d)(2) Full appeal + State fair hearing — 438 Subpart F

Casting guardrail: Maria is not positioned as the “vulnerable member who gets denied,” and her thread is deliberately decoupled from the seed §1557 disparate-impact cohort. Default demo path is the save (Path A). If the demo is tribal-facing, vet the name and framing with tribal partners before it ships.

Honesty ledger — what’s real, what we’re proposing, what’s illustrative

Real — reused engine
  • The 11-stage sealed thread (EMR → surveillance) with per-stage decision + audit record.
  • The pend + records-request kickback (277-RFAI) loop.
  • Deemed-adverse fire with its floor — it still fires at full autonomy (pDeemedTimeout).
  • The NABD is issued by a human (UM), never an agent auto-deny (human:UM).
  • Twin-Ladder verdict per stage — permitted rung, adverse capped at A1 (“Advise”), submission gate.
  • Hash-chained append-only ledger + live per-entry re-verify; NIST per-record tag.
Proposed — build for this showcase
  • The member-anchored single thread as the spine (today F6 fires across the book, not on one named member).
  • A clock-jeopardy detector. Today only the NIST tag DEEMED-ADVERSE-TIMEOUT exists in nistMap; it is not in the 40-algorithm library and mints no ticket.
  • The acceleration ticket → UM ops, pre-loaded RCA + recommendation + verdict.
  • A distinct member-appeal / fair-hearing pathway (the engine’s recovery lane is for provider payment disputes, not member appeals).
  • The member-facing NABD + §1557 language-access beat, and the oversight signal back to the plan/State.
Illustrative — labeled
  • Maria and the provider are fictional and PHI-safe; member/provider names carry “(illustrative).”
  • Mock channel — nothing transmitted.
  • Clinical specifics kept abstract; no diagnosis narrative.
  • Clock cadence is illustrative pacing.
  • Gold-carding is a commercial/state-statute construct, shown only where a plan adopts it via policy.
  • NIST alignment is demonstrated by the log — not a certification.

Path B · the safety net — an honest failure, contained

The clock expires anyway

This is a plan failure — a constructive denial by operation of law. No human chose it. We do not dress it up as governance working. What the safeguard guarantees is that the failure is caught, attributed, and impossible to hide:

  • A human (UM) issues a proper NABD — never an agent, never mislabeled as a clinical denial.
  • Member-facing notice with §1557 language access (interpreter, taglines).
  • Appeal + State fair hearing rights, incl. deemed exhaustion — 438.408(c)(3) / (f)(1)(i).
  • The miss itself becomes an oversight signal back to plan-performance / program integrity for the State.

Note: new-PA case → aid-paid-pending does not attach (it would only on a reduction/termination of an existing authorization).

How the coalition hardened this — the red-team, applied

Before drawing a pixel we put the draft to the adversarial reviewer. It returned twelve ranked findings and five gaps. The load-bearing ones — and exactly what changed in this v1 — are below. (This is the “show thinking” you asked for: the story is stronger because it was attacked first.)

HIGH

Draft: “even at full autonomy a human owns the ‘no’ — that’s the floor” — applied to the deemed-adverse ending.

Fixed: a deemed-adverse is a constructive denial by operation of law — no human chose it. Path B is now framed as an honest plan failure, contained, not a governance win. “A human owns the ‘no’” is reserved for the clinical MD denial, where it’s literally true.

HIGH

Draft: the one AI/AN member is the safety-net “denial-risk protagonist,” on the same FQHC that carries the seed §1557 disparate-impact ticket.

Fixed: equity recast as protective infrastructure working for her; Path A (save) is the default; her thread is decoupled from the disparate-impact cohort; voluntary MCO enrollment + I/T/U access stated; name/casting flagged for tribal-partner vetting.

HIGH

Draft: “position recovery” routed Maria’s member appeal through the recovery lane.

Fixed: the engine’s recovery lane is for provider payment disputes, not member appeals. A distinct member-appeal / fair-hearing pathway is called out as Proposed; “recovery” for Path A now means recovered timeliness/access, not money.

HIGH

Draft: “DEEMED-ADVERSE-TIMEOUT already in the 40-algorithm library,” with a seeded ticket to Dr. Patel.

Fixed: only the NIST tag exists today; the detector, the ticket, and the routing are Proposed, not built. The honesty ledger now says so.

HIGH

Draft: adverse actions “capped at A2.”

Fixed: the engine floors adverse to A1 (“Advise — a human decides”) — a stronger guardrail than the draft claimed. Corrected everywhere. Better story: on anything adverse the machine can only advise.

MED

Draft: the 72-hour clock “starts at the pend,” and CMS-0057-F is the source of it.

Fixed: clock starts at receipt (Beat 5); the pend consumes it. Maria’s 72h anchored to 438.210(d)(2); CMS-0057-F cited only as the standard-clock context; drugs excluded, so her service is medical.

MED

Gaps: no member-facing notice / language access; no continuity-of-care clarity; no oversight signal on the miss; Part 2 silent.

Added: §1557 language-access on the NABD; aid-paid-pending clarified (doesn’t attach on a new PA); the miss now feeds a plan-performance oversight signal; Part 2 honored on any SUD-adjacent record pull.
