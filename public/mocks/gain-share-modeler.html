Assumptions & honesty rules baked into the engine

This is a deal-modeling tool, so the traps that flatter a bad deal are wired in as guardrails, not footnotes.

  • Holistic cohorts blend member-weighted. Each whole-person segment (clinical risk + behavioral + social) carries its own size, benchmark, savings rate and variability; deal outcomes are the member-weighted blend, and the breakdown shows which cohort drives savings vs downside. Savings and risk concentrate — a blended panel average hides that.
  • Credibility scales the bar. A small cohort isn't statistically credible, so its required MSR is raised by a credibility factor (∝ √members). Sub-credible cohorts are flagged — you can't book savings off 400 members as if they were 40,000.
  • Fairness watch on SDOH cohorts. High-SDOH / dual-eligible segments are flagged (Section 1557): savings there must not come from under-service, and disparate-impact must be monitored. The tool surfaces the watch; it does not let SDOH silently drive an adverse split.
  • Recovery is quarantined and decays under capitation (κ 1.0→0.75→0.50→0.20→~0). It can never enter gross savings or the provider payout.
  • MSR is a step function (first-dollar-after-gate by default; a share-above-threshold toggle is provided).
  • Expected ≠ max downside on two-sided / sub-cap / global budget — both shown; max exposure is capped by stop-loss / reserves / reinsurance and honours the loss-share and cohort variability.
  • Rebasing tax (realized-savings ratchet only) shown against a no-rebase line; multi-year is NPV-discounted.
  • Capitation reframes the payer's gain — under global budget, per-claim + recovery ≈ 0; payer value = risk transfer + predictability + baked-in discount.
  • Phantom-savings guard and MLR-floor rebate (directional proxy) fire on payer net.

Known gaps for a production risk tool (not modeled here)

  • Probability of hitting the savings rate — point estimate; a real underwriting tool needs an outcome distribution and a tail (P95 loss) per cohort.
  • Attribution churn across cohorts — members migrate between segments and MCOs mid-period.
  • Claims runout / IBNR, reinsurance premium, high-cost-claimant outlier pooling, working-capital timing.
