Governance · the interlock

Evidence gates authority. Stronger evidence — not a bolder setting — is what unlocks autonomy.

The two ladders move independently. The agent’s permitted rung is always the weakest link between them, so weak evidence holds a highly-autonomous agent back, by design.

Ledger Intelligence · the analyst workbench

Analysts don’t run a fixed checklist — they ask the ledger a question, and a governed codegen pipeline answers it.

What’s real

Real & running today

  • Reconciliation, the Twin-Ladder interlock, and recovery-draft logic are production code — this page runs the same rules.
  • A governed agent drafts the recovery and suspends at a human gate; no payer-facing submission auto-executes at any tier.
  • The Evidence Record is append-only and tamper-evident; the dual-party projection and escalation-signal seams behind the ledger view are real, tested, and gate-green.
