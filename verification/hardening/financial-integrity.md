# Financial and Actuarial Integrity Hardening (R1 red-team)

Scope: would the money paths survive a CMS / actuarial / RADV audit and produce correct payment?
Read-only findings. Branches assessed: (1) risk adjustment, (2) encounter-data integrity, (3) RADV
defensibility, (4) claims / payment correctness, (5) TCOC / PMPM / MLR derivation, (6) financial
reconciliation. This doc is the money-integrity lens; it complements `reconciliation.md` (REC-05/06/07/11
overlap and are cross-referenced) rather than repeating it.

## Headline

The claims mapping is standards-literate at *capture* time: the financial chain
`Claim <-ADJUDICATED_BY- ClaimResponse <-EXPLAINED_BY- EOB` is modeled with correct semantics, CARC/RARC
are captured, and the X12 CARC GROUP is turned into a categorical member-liability disposition
(`carcGroup.ts`, the prior R1-I7-6 fix). But everything DOWNSTREAM of capture that actually earns or
protects money is absent. There is no risk-adjustment submission (EDPS / RAPS / Medicaid encounters), no
acceptance/rejection reconciliation (999 / 277CA / MAO-002), no RADV audit trail from a submitted
diagnosis back to a signed source document and an attesting provider, no way to RETRACT an unsupported
HCC (there is no submission to retract from and no diagnosis void path), no COB, no duplicate / overpayment
/ FWA detection, and no 835 remittance. The HCC classifier (`terminology/classify/hccClassify.ts`) is
wired to NOTHING in the app. The headline TCOC / PMPM / RAF numbers the product sells on
(`smartFhirMockData.ts`, `mockData.data.patients.ts`) are hardcoded demo strings, not derived from
adjudicated dollars. In short: the platform can DESCRIBE a claim, but it cannot yet EARN a risk-adjusted
dollar, PROVE that dollar to an auditor, or COMPUTE the cost metric it claims to bend.

| FIN-01 | RADV / risk adjustment | No source-document / MEAT linkage on a captured HCC. `conditions.ts` copies `hcc` + `hccRelevant` off the source event as plain node properties; nothing ties the diagnosis to a specific date-of-service, encounter type, rendering-provider NPI, or a signed clinical note. `verificationStatus` defaults to `'confirmed'`. The graph holds a diagnosis with no artifact that SUBSTANTIATES it. | RADV validates every sampled HCC against the medical record (MEAT: Monitored/Evaluated/Assessed/Treated). A diagnosis with no linkable substantiation is an automatic RADV deletion and an extrapolated takeback; submitting it is False Claims Act exposure. The single most consequential risk-adjustment audit gap. | Critical | I8A / I12 (diagnosis substantiation ledger) |
| FIN-03 | RISK ADJUSTMENT | An unsupported HCC cannot be RETRACTED before submission. There is no diagnosis void / delete / suppression flow, no `entered-in-error` propagation, and no submission state on a diagnosis. A coder or auditor who finds an HCC unsupported has no mechanism to pull it, because there is neither a submission to amend nor a delete edge. | The core internal-audit control of risk adjustment (drop what you cannot defend before you file it) does not exist. Unsupported HCCs flow through uncorrected, converting a fixable coding error into a filed false claim. | Critical | I8A (diagnosis lifecycle + delete/void) |
| FIN-06 | ENCOUNTER DATA INTEGRITY | No encounter-submission pipeline of any kind. Nothing generates EDPS 837 encounter submissions (MA), Medicaid encounter files, or legacy RAPS from adjudicated claims / diagnoses. The platform INGESTS claims and never SUBMITS encounters upstream. | For an MA / Medicaid plan the encounter submission IS the risk-revenue mechanism and a CMS/state compliance obligation. Its total absence means no risk revenue is actually earned and encounter-completeness mandates are unmet. | Critical | I12 (EDPS / RAPS / Medicaid encounter pipeline) |
| FIN-07 | ENCOUNTER DATA INTEGRITY | No acceptance / rejection reconciliation (999 / 277CA / MAO-002). Revenue leaks with no alarm and no correction loop, and the plan cannot prove submission completeness to CMS. | Critical | I12 (EDI ack + return-file reconciliation) |
| FIN-10 | RADV DEFENSIBILITY | No audit trail from a submitted diagnosis to substantiating evidence + provider attestation. The evidence ledger (`goldenThread` / `evidence`) threads financial-clearance and PA, not risk-adjustment diagnoses. Nothing produces the RADV artifact: HCC -> the specific encounter -> the signed progress note -> the attesting provider. | RADV and OIG audits demand exactly this chain per sampled HCC. Without it the plan cannot defend a single sampled diagnosis and faces full extrapolated recovery across the contract. | Critical | I8A / I12 (RADV evidence packet) |
| FIN-15 | TCOC / PMPM / MLR | TCOC / PMPM / MLR figures are asserted demo numbers, not derived from adjudicated amounts. `smartFhirMockData.ts` hardcodes "PMPM $1,240 vs $780 target" and "RAF delta +0.12 / $12,400 value"; `mockData.data.patients.ts` / `providers.ts` hardcode `pmpmCost` / `pmpmTarget`. | FIN-02 | RISK ADJUSTMENT | No supplemental / data-source-type provenance on a diagnosis. CMS treats encounter-derived, claim-derived, and chart-review / HRA / supplemental diagnoses very differently; a chart-review-only add with no linked encounter is the classic RADV takeback. `conditions.ts` has no field distinguishing these, and `hccClassify` stamps the model version but never the source-of-truth. | The plan cannot separate defensible encounter-backed HCCs from at-risk supplemental adds, so it cannot self-assess RADV exposure or purge risky chart-review-only codes before filing. | High | I8A (diagnosis source-type provenance) |
| FIN-04 | RISK ADJUSTMENT | RAF / risk score is never computed. `hccClassify.ts` (diagnosis -> HCC group) is imported only by the terminology index and tests, called by NO app path. There is no coefficient application, no demographic / disease-interaction terms, no member or population RAF rollup. RAF value exists only as a hardcoded mock string. | Without a real RAF calculation the plan cannot forecast risk revenue, prioritize suspects by dollar value, size RADV exposure, or reconcile expected vs paid risk revenue. The actuarial core of the product is a stub. | High | I8A / I12 (RAF engine) |
| FIN-05 | RISK ADJUSTMENT | HCC suspects have no capture -> review -> confirm/reject -> submit -> accept lifecycle. A suspect is surfaced as a display string; there is no state machine, no coder confirm/reject with actor + timestamp, no record of which suspects were adopted vs dropped. | An unauditable suspect list invites over-capture (compliance risk) and leaves adopted diagnoses with no confirmation provenance, feeding FIN-01/FIN-10. No governance over what gets submitted. | High | I8A (suspect adjudication workflow) |
| FIN-08 | ENCOUNTER DATA INTEGRITY | No claim -> encounter completeness reconciliation. Nothing diffs the set of adjudicated claims against the set of submitted / accepted encounters, so dropped encounters (a known material revenue leak) are undetectable. Distinct from REC-05 (orphan ClaimResponse hold), which is un-run in production anyway. | Encounter under-submission directly suppresses risk revenue with no detection; the plan cannot attest that every reimbursable service became a submitted encounter. | High | I8A / I12 (claim-to-encounter tie-out) |
| FIN-09 | CLAIMS / ENCOUNTER | No correction / resubmission (replacement / void) path. No 837 frequency-code 7 (replace) / 8 (void) handling, and no claim void / reversal (register I7 flagged claim void/reversal absent). A corrected diagnosis cannot be resubmitted, and a voided claim neither reverses its financial chain nor removes its HCC from risk. | Errors are permanent: an overstated claim or an unsupported diagnosis, once captured, cannot be walked back through the money or risk paths. Blocks both overpayment correction and RADV self-correction. | High | I8A / I12 (void / replacement handling) |
| FIN-11 | RADV DEFENSIBILITY | Provider attestation for risk adjustment not modeled. `providerRef` resolves a BILLING NPI on a claim, but no rendering-provider attestation, signature, or credential type (MD/DO/NP acceptability) is captured on a diagnosis. | RADV deletes diagnoses from non-acceptable provider types / specialties and unsigned encounters. Without rendering-provider credentialing on the diagnosis the plan cannot pre-screen these deletions. | High | I8A (rendering-provider attestation) |
| FIN-12 | CLAIMS / PAYMENT / FWA | Adjudication outcome and `paymentAmount` are trusted as-is; no duplicate-claim, overpayment, or FWA detection. No dedupe of resubmitted claims (same member / provider / DOS / code), no duplicate-pay or overpayment check, no FWA anomaly signals, and `paymentAmount` is never validated against billed / allowed. | Duplicate payments, overpayments, and fraud/waste/abuse patterns are undetectable. Payment-integrity is a primary payer cost lever and is entirely absent. | High | I8 / I8A (payment integrity + FWA) |
| FIN-13 | COB / PAYMENT | No coordination of benefits / order of benefits. No `Coverage.order`, no primary/secondary sequencing, no allowed-vs-paid coordination (register R1-I6-5, REC-11). `deriveMemberLiability` computes liability as if the plan is always primary. | For any member with secondary coverage, member liability, plan-paid, and therefore TCOC are computed wrong. COB errors are a top source of payer over/under-payment and member abrasion. | High | I8A (COB sequencing) |
| FIN-16 | TCOC / PMPM / MLR | No premium / capitation / revenue side, so MLR and risk-adjusted TCOC are uncomputable. Even if paid amounts were aggregated (FIN-15), there is no premium or capitation ingestion and no RAF normalization (FIN-04), so MLR (the regulated 85% floor) and risk-adjusted PMPM cannot be produced. | The plan cannot compute or monitor the regulated MLR, nor normalize PMPM for acuity, so it cannot prove rebate compliance or compare cost fairly across risk. | High | I12 (premium ingest + MLR) |
| FIN-17 | FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION | No claims-vs-encounters-vs-payments-vs-golden-thread tie-out; held orphan claims leak. `projectClaimsWithIntegrity` / `holdOrphanClaims` are called by nothing in production (REC-05), there is no 835 remittance parser (REC-07, certification matrix marks 835 partial), and no three-way claim -> remit -> payment reconciliation exists. | Adjudicated-vs-paid mismatches, underpayments, and permanently incomplete financial chains are undetectable and silent. The plan cannot prove adjudicated equals remitted equals paid. | High | I8A / I12 (835 ingest + 3-way tie-out) |
| FIN-14 | CLAIMS / PAYMENT | Member-liability derivation is coarse (group-presence only), not dollar-apportioned. `deriveMemberLiability` returns member-responsibility / not / indeterminate from CARC GROUP PRESENCE; it never apportions the PR dollar amount per CARC line. `ClaimResponse.paymentAmount` and `EobPayload.paymentAmount` are captured independently and never tied to each other (REC-07). | Even when groups are present the actual member-owed dollar figure is not computed, so member cost-share, EOB accuracy, and TCOC member-liability split are directional only, not exact. | Med | I8A (per-line liability apportionment) |
